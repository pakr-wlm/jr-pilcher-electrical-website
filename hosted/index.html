Reliable Electricians, With Unmatched Experience

J.R. Pilcher has been in the electrical and electronics field for about 52 years. He started in the U.S. Air Force, studying electronic warfare systems for four years. After the service he went into the plastics industry, where he learned industrial electricity, control systems, troubleshooting and repair.

After 17 years in plastics he earned his Master Electrician license and started his own business in 1993. Customers know him as the man to call when nobody else can fix it, and he works on anything that has electricity in it, including battery-powered equipment.
